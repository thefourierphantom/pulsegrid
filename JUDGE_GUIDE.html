Backend Algorithms
Weighted Composite Scoring — 5 signals (latency drift, error creep, retry amplify, timeout cluster, resource pressure) combined at fixed weights; multi-signal amplifier 1.20× at 3+ signals, 1.35× at 4+
Graph Blast Radius — 2-hop propagation on weighted service dependency graph; each edge transfers (score − 0.30) × weight × 0.50 upstream
Sigmoid Telemetry Simulation — seeded RNG (noise_seed=42) + sigmoid curves for realistic degradation trajectories per scenario
Chain Diagnostic Engine — response signals scored against SCENARIO_SIGNALS matrix; best-match populates all 7 chain layers with scenario-specific mitigations
7 Recommendation Patterns — rule-based engine matches system state + scenario to immediate/optimize actions with rationale
